<MarketingPrivacyChoices>

With your permission, Expanso loads a US Browser Speed visitor identity matching tag for marketing and advertising on this examples site. Accepting analytics does not enable this tag. Marketing is off until you choose “Allow marketing” here.

The tag contacts the vendor, which receives your IP address and browser request headers and may set or read third-party cookies to match visits to an identity for advertising. Your browser may block those cookies. We send no page URL in the referrer for this request.

We remember this choice on this site for up to one year. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track block the tag even if you previously allowed marketing. You can return here through “Marketing privacy choices” in the footer at any time.

Choose “Decline marketing” to withdraw permission. If the tag has loaded, the page reloads to stop its code and prevent future requests. Other open tabs recheck once a second and when you return to them; your browser may delay checks in inactive tabs. Withdrawal cannot recall a request already sent, erase data the vendor received, or delete its third-party cookies. Use your browser’s cookie controls to remove those cookies. See the [Expanso privacy policy](https://expanso.io/privacy) for privacy rights and contact details.

</MarketingPrivacyChoices>
